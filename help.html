Help & Safety

Staying safe

Meet in a public place, ideally during daytime, for any handover.

Inspect the item carefully before paying — don't rely on photos alone.

Avoid advance payments, especially to sellers who aren't verified.

Keep the conversation in Marketplace chat until you're ready to meet — it keeps a record if something goes wrong.

Raise a concern

Tell us what happened — reports about scams, unsafe behavior, or platform bugs go straight to our trust & safety queue.
